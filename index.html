Materials & care

Shell: 80% cotton, 20% polyester brushed fleece. Lining: 100% polyester sherpa. Hood: premium modacrylic/acrylic long-pile faux fur.

Turn inside out, cold delicate wash, no fabric softener. Lay flat to dry — never tumble or iron the fur. Revive the pile with a soft brush.

Shipping

Every piece is made to order: 10–14 business days in the atelier, then complimentary tracked express shipping in the U.S. (2–5 business days). International express is a flat $35 (5–10 business days). Duties and taxes may apply on delivery outside the U.S.

Returns

Because each hoodie is made for you, orders are final sale once production begins. You can change or cancel within 24 hours of ordering. If anything arrives with a defect, write to us within 14 days of delivery and we will repair or remake it.

07 — Questions

Good toknow.

Is the fur real?

No — and on purpose. We use premium long-pile faux fur (a dense modacrylic/acrylic blend, around 30mm pile). It looks and feels luxurious, is consistent from piece to piece, is easier to care for, and can be shipped anywhere without restrictions.

When will my hoodie arrive?

Each piece is made to order in 10–14 business days, then ships tracked express: 2–5 business days in the U.S., 5–10 internationally. You'll receive a confirmation from PayPal right away and tracking when it ships.

How does it fit?

Relaxed and unisex with a dropped shoulder. Take your usual size for the intended fit, or size down for a closer cut. The sherpa adds warmth, not bulk. See the size guide for measurements.

How can I pay?

Checkout is powered by PayPal: pay with your PayPal balance, Venmo, PayPal Pay Later (pay in 4 or monthly), or any major debit or credit card — no PayPal account needed.

Can I return or exchange?

As every piece is made for you, orders are final sale once production begins. You can change your size or colourway, or cancel, within 24 hours of ordering. Anything defective is repaired or remade — just write to us within 14 days of delivery.

How do I care for the fur?

Cold delicate wash inside out, no softener, and dry flat — never tumble dry or iron. Brush the hood gently with a soft brush to lift the pile.
